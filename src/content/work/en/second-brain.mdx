## The Context

Every AI conversation started from zero. The most concrete proof was my internship applications: for every opening, I adapted my CV, and the AI helping me didn't know me, or was out of date. I re-explained my context, my experience, my projects, whatever matched the role. When I forgot a detail, I went back to another AI to find it. My profile was scattered across several AIs, each with its own version of me. I wanted my knowledge base to be **reachable by any AI** (ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, opencode), not locked in one tool.

## What I Built

A self-hosted "second brain": an **Obsidian vault** as the source of truth, versioned on a **private GitHub repo**, indexed by an **n8n** pipeline into **Qdrant** (vector store), embedded locally with **Ollama bge-m3**, and generated with **DeepSeek V4 Flash Vision** (via the OpenCode Go gateway). Everything is exposed to any AI through a **dedicated MCP server**, authenticated with **OAuth**.

**Three MCP tools:**

## What it's useful for (beyond applications)

The CV was the trigger, but the same base serves many other things: preparing an interview ("summarize project X and my technical choices"), finding an architecture decision in my notes, having the AI dive into a project's source, or feeding my articles. Each time, it's time saved by not re-explaining or digging around.

And above all: the AI knows **how I develop** (structure, naming, patterns, best practices). On a new project, it applies what I've already done well elsewhere, instead of producing generic code.

## What This Experience Taught Me

Shipping a production MCP server surfaced several hard-won lessons: **OpenAI model nodes don't expose custom headers** (the OpenCode Go gateway requires `x-opencode-session`), call the API directly over HTTP; **splitInBatches aborts on empty items**, prefer linear pipelines; **uncontrolled AI writes are a real threat**, the quarantine-by-status pattern is the simplest effective gate; and **re-indexing is not idempotent by default**, deterministic IDs (content hash) make ingestion duplicate-free, differential and near-instant.
